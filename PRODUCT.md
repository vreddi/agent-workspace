# Product

**Agent Workspace** — an AI-assisted task manager where your agents are
characters in a cozy pixel village. You capture tasks; your agents (each with
a name, face, and personality) will file, remind, and eventually work on them.

## Register

`product` — the authenticated app is a tool people live in daily. Design
serves the task; the village canvas is the one place charm leads.

## Users

Built **neurodivergent-first** (ADHD, autism, anxiety-prone users), which
makes it better for everyone. The people using this lose whole afternoons to
cluttered dashboards. They need one clear thing to look at, not a cockpit.

## Strategic principles

1. **One focus per screen.** Every screen has a single primary thing and a
   single primary action. Everything else is quiet or a click away.
2. **Calm is the feature.** Competing apps win by louder; we win by quieter.
   If a screen feels like a notification center, it has failed.
3. **The village is the heart.** Agents-as-characters is the emotional hook:
   warmth and personality live in the pixel world, not in UI chrome.
4. **No fake UI.** Nothing renders unless it works. No dummy badges,
   placeholder alerts, or decorative status dots — invented urgency is
   hostile to our users.
5. **Progressive disclosure.** Start minimal; reveal detail on intent
   (hover, click, dedicated pages). Never all-at-once.

## Tone

Plain, warm, brief. Say "3 tasks today", not "You have 3 high-leverage
opportunities". Personality belongs to the agents (their dialogue lines),
not to system copy.

## Anti-references

- SaaS analytics dashboards (metric grids, sparklines, "insights" pills).
- Gamified to-do apps (streaks, confetti, XP). The village is cozy, not a
  slot machine.
- AI-forward chrome (typewriter effects, glowing "✦ thinking…" pills).
